SPRINT 10A-4 — DRIVER SELF-SERVICE PORTAL

Goal:

Allow drivers to operate daily activities directly from WhatsApp and self-service portal without admin intervention.

Do not replace Fleet Foundation.

Reuse:

* fleet_drivers
* fleet_units
* fleet_fuel_logs
* fleet_maintenance_records
* fleet_documents
* fleet_utilization_logs
* driver_memory_snapshots
* ai_tasks
* WhatsApp command framework

====================================================
A. DRIVER PORTAL
================

Public routes:

/driver/home/:token
/driver/profile/:token
/driver/documents/:token
/driver/trips/:token
/driver/history/:token

Token based.

No login required.

Token bound to driver.

====================================================
B. DRIVER ONBOARDING
====================

WhatsApp command:

DAFTAR DRIVER

Generate 72h token.

Driver completes:

* name
* phone
* license number
* license expiry
* emergency contact
* assigned vehicle

Create/update fleet_drivers.

====================================================
C. DRIVER DOCUMENTS
===================

Upload:

* SIM
* KTP
* Medical certificate
* Driver photo

Use DocumentValidationEngine.

Status:

valid
incomplete
invalid
needs_review

Store audit trail.
