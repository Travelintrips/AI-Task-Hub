====================================================

- document compliance score
- maintenance score
- fuel efficiency score
- driver score
- age condition score
- utilization score

Weighted:
document 25%
maintenance 25%
fuel 15%
driver 15%
age 10%
utilization 10%

Risk level:
80–100 LOW
60–79 MEDIUM
40–59 HIGH
0–39 CRITICAL

Create AI task if:
- risk level HIGH
- risk level CRITICAL
