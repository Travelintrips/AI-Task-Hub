SPRINT 10B-2 — COMPANY ONBOARDING FACTORY

Goal:

Allow a new company to become operational in less than 15 minutes.

Scope:

Company level only.

Do NOT build:

* branches
* divisions
* departments
* cost centers

Those belong to Sprint 10C.

====================================================
A. COMPANY CREATION WIZARD
==========================

Create:

/company-onboarding

Steps:

1. Company Profile
2. Admin User
3. WhatsApp Setup
4. Module Selection
5. Initial Data
6. Verification
7. Go Live

====================================================
B. COMPANY PROFILE
==================

Collect:

company_name
industry
phone
email
website
address

Store:

company_settings

====================================================
C. ADMIN USER
=============

Create first:

owner
company_admin

Generate:

temporary password
activation link

====================================================
D. MODULE SELECTION
===================

Allow enable:

AI Task Center
Fleet
Purchasing
CRM
Vendor Portal
Driver Portal
Executive Command

Store:

company_modules

====================================================
E. INITIAL DATA SEED
====================

Optional:

customers
vendors
fleet units
team members

Methods:

manual
csv upload
skip

====================================================
F. READINESS CHECK
==================

Verify:

company profile
admin exists
WA configured
minimum module configured

Output:

readiness %

====================================================
G. GO LIVE
==========

When readiness >= 80%

Generate:

welcome checklist
first executive briefing
activation audit log

====================================================
H. COMPANY TEMPLATE
===================

Create templates:

Logistics Company
Trading Company
Sport Center
Tenant Management

Each template:

preselect modules
default settings
starter intents

====================================================
I. GOVERNANCE INTEGRATION
=========================

Company Governance Center:

show

* onboarding progress
* readiness
* activation date
* active modules
